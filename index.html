⚽ GET THE BALL TO LEE!

Baiyun Wanderers · 7-a-side tactical challenge · Three difficulty levels

Pass, send teammates running into space away from defenders, and beat the moving opposition. Get the ball to Lee in as few passes as possible. Passes at 60% or below are locked; 61–74% passes are risky attempts; passes at 75% or above are guaranteed. Use runs to create better passing lanes! You can also manually position individual teammates.

Passes at 60% or below are locked. Passes from 61–74% can be attempted, but may be intercepted. Passes at 75% or above always succeed. Ratings combine the passer’s passing skill, receiver’s running skill, distance, and the opposition’s interception strength and position. Run into Space moves the whole team. Use Move Teammate Manually to select an off-ball Wanderer and tap a destination on the pitch. The player runs as far as their rating allows; the opposition reacts after each move. Move into space to improve your chances before risking a pass.

Match commentary

Wanderers ratings are randomised for fun (3–10). Use the 🎲 button to shuffle all ratings and reset scores, lineup and attack. Opposition interception ratings depend on the selected difficulty. Switching opponents starts a fresh attack, but keeps your goals and player ratings. Ratings and outcomes are game mechanics, not real match predictions.
